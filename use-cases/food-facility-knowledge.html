Food facility investigation knowledge assistant

Bring regulations, facility history, HACCP plans, sampling guidance, and traceability procedures into one cited investigation view.

Imagine when complaints and laboratory signals point to a food processing facility.

The investigation team must understand the product and process, review prior violations and HACCP controls, identify the right sampling plan, coordinate traceability, and determine which state and federal authorities apply before entering the facility.

Business need and data challenge

Relevant evidence is distributed across food code, state regulation, federal guidance, facility permits, process-flow diagrams, HACCP plans, prior inspections, complaint records, laboratory sampling manuals, and product-distribution records. Different versions, terminology, and jurisdiction create risk during a time-sensitive investigation.

The business need is fast access to the right cited requirement and investigation procedure—not an automated compliance or enforcement decision.

Potential outcomes

  • Faster preparation for complex facility investigations.
  • More consistent use of current cited requirements and procedures.
  • Clearer links among complaints, controls, samples, findings, and follow-up.

How this addresses the challenge

Versioned, permission-aware retrieval narrows a large regulatory and facility record set to the sources relevant to the product, hazard, process, and jurisdiction. Citations keep the evidence visible while authorized staff retain responsibility for interpretation and action.

How this applies across the broader workflow

  • Record the complaint cluster, laboratory signal, product, lot, distribution, process step, and jurisdiction during investigation intake.
  • Retrieve the applicable food code, permit, inspection history, HACCP plan, preventive controls, sanitation procedures, environmental monitoring, and sampling guidance to build the field plan.
  • Tie post-visit findings to observed evidence and cited authority while laboratory results, enforcement referrals, corrective actions, traceability, and reinspection remain in their systems of record.
