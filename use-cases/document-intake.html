USE CASE 01 · SURVEILLANCE & INFORMATICS

Case and laboratory document intake

Convert incoming reports into review-ready surveillance records while preserving the original source and human validation.

Related Azure AI App TemplateMulti-modal Content Processing

Extracts information from unstructured documents and maps it to a structured format.

View template

Business need

Scanned forms, faxes, PDFs, and laboratory attachments create intake delays and repetitive data entry. The goal is faster preparation for staff review—not autonomous case creation.

Data involved

Case-report forms, laboratory PDFs, demographics, onset and report dates, provider and facility, specimen and result fields, plus source, confidence, reviewer, and audit metadata.

01Capture

Receive securely and retain the original.

02Classify

Select the correct document schema.

03Extract

Read fields with source and confidence.

04Validate

Staff confirm or correct the draft.

05Load

Post approved data and monitor quality.

Applied scenarios

  • Prepare a faxed disease report for surveillance entry.
  • Associate a laboratory PDF with a case and propose specimen fields.
  • Process a historical backlog while separating low-confidence records.

Required guardrails

  • Process protected health information only in approved environments.
  • Require authorized staff acceptance before finalizing a record.
  • Keep every value traceable to the original document.
  • Monitor correction rates by document type and field.
