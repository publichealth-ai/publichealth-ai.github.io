USE CASE 06 · SURVEILLANCE & INFORMATICS

Cross-agency surveillance knowledge hub

Search partner guidance across organizational boundaries without bypassing the permissions attached to each source.

Related Azure AI App TemplateAI application with SharePoint knowledge and actions

Uses SharePoint semantic retrieval and its built-in access controls for grounded agent responses.

View template

Imagine when a multijurisdictional foodborne cluster crosses three counties.

A state informatician needs the current reporting protocol, laboratory submission instructions, interview guidance, partner roles, and escalation contacts. Each item is owned by a different program or agency with different access rules.

Business need and data challenge

Partner knowledge lives in separate SharePoint libraries, Teams-connected sites, portals, and document repositories. Permissions may be inherited from Entra ID groups, while data-use agreements restrict who can see investigation or laboratory procedures.

The business need is unified discovery across authorized sources without copying restricted documents into a broadly accessible index.

How this applies across the broader workflow

The user signs in with organizational identity. SharePoint and source-system permissions determine which libraries, folders, and documents can be retrieved. The query searches authorized state reporting rules, local investigation SOPs, public health laboratory submission guides, emergency plans, and partner agreements.

The answer preserves each source's agency, document owner, effective date, and citation rather than blending requirements into one unattributed response. Restricted line lists, laboratory records, or case data stay in their systems of record; the hub can link authorized users to them but does not duplicate them into the knowledge index.

Questions for your team

  • Which repositories are authoritative for each topic?
  • How are partner access groups granted and revoked?
  • What information must never enter the shared index?

Named data and processes

  • SharePoint libraries, Teams-connected sites, reporting protocols, laboratory guides, plans, data-use agreements, Entra ID groups, and sensitivity labels.
  • Identity verification, authorization, semantic retrieval, citation, source-owner escalation, access review, and audit reporting.
  • Repository, document owner, version, permission group, sensitivity, citation, query, and access decision.

Required guardrails

  • Apply security before retrieval, not after generation.
  • Keep source owners responsible for permissions and currency.
  • Test that users cannot infer restricted content.
  • Align access logs with approved retention policy.
