ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH

Environmental health field inspection assistant

Guide swimming-pool and other facility inspections with current requirements, structured evidence capture, and review-ready follow-up.

Imagine when seasonal swimming-pool inspections must be completed before opening weekend.

The field schedule spans dozens of facilities. Each visit requires the current permit, prior violations, water-chemistry records, equipment observations, photographs, code requirements, and follow-up deadlines—even when connectivity is unreliable.

Business need and data challenge

Inspection data is split across permit systems, prior inspection PDFs, complaint records, facility plans, paper logs, photographs, and state or local code. Facility identifiers and addresses may differ across systems, while measurements and evidence need time, location, and inspector attribution.

The business need is a consistent field workflow that reduces after-visit documentation without automating the inspection finding or enforcement decision.

Plan visits

Schedule pools using permits, history, complaints, and risk

Inspect facility

The inspector records measurements, observations, and photos

Process evidence

AI extracts logs, labels, permits, and supporting documents

Make finding

The inspector validates evidence and applies cited requirements

Correct and close

Issue notice, track correction, reinspect, and close

Where the accelerator fits

Use Azure AI for evidence processing and Power Platform for the inspection workflow.

Multi-modal Content Processing can extract structured values from uploaded logs, labels, permits, and supporting documents, but it is not a field-inspection application and does not provide offline checklists, measurements, signatures, inspection scheduling, notices, or reinspection. Those workflow capabilities belong in Power Apps and Power Automate around the accelerator.

Reuse: the multimodal document and image-processing pattern, structured output, and review experience.

Adapt: facility and permit data, environmental-health checklists, offline behavior, inspection evidence, code citations, Power Platform connectors, and the reinspection workflow.

Keep outside: the inspector's finding, enforcement action, immediate closure decision, and any legal notice that requires authorized approval.

Potential outcomes

  • More complete and consistent inspections across jurisdictions and staff.
  • Less after-visit transcription and faster notice preparation.
  • Clearer facility risk, repeat violation, and reinspection visibility.

How this addresses the challenge

The mobile workflow connects the facility record, checklist, measurements, photos, and cited requirement at the point of inspection. AI processes supporting evidence, while staff validate every proposed value and retain responsibility for findings and enforcement.

How this applies across the broader workflow

  • Assemble the permit, previous inspection, unresolved violations, complaint history, facility plan, applicable code, and route before the visit.
  • Record disinfectant residual, pH, water clarity, safety equipment, operator logs, barriers, signage, photos, timestamps, and corrective actions during the inspection.
  • Confirm evidence and cited requirements after synchronization, then trigger notice preparation, corrective-action tracking, reinspection, supervisor review, closure, and risk-based planning.
