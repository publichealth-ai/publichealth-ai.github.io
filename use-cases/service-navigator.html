USE CASE 04 · COMMUNITY SERVICES

Public health service navigator

Give residents one conversational entry point to current clinics, testing, vaccination, alerts, and other local health services.

Related Azure AI App TemplateCustomer Chatbot Solution Accelerator

Uses an orchestrator and specialized agents to answer grounded service and policy questions.

View template

Business need

Residents must navigate fragmented information about programs, locations, requirements, and schedules. A navigator combines approved guidance with live service data to provide a clear next step.

Data involved

Service catalogs, eligibility guidance, required documents, clinic locations and hours, appointment availability, accessibility, language support, advisories, and approved service APIs.

01Understand

Identify service, location, and access needs.

02Retrieve

Find current approved guidance.

03Connect

Call location or scheduling services.

04Respond

Give a plain-language next step.

05Hand off

Escalate urgent or uncertain requests.

Applied scenarios

  • Find a nearby testing location with current hours and requirements.
  • Show relevant vaccination services without making a clinical recommendation.
  • Explain locally applicable steps after a public health advisory.

Required guardrails

  • Separate stable guidance from live availability data.
  • Do not diagnose or recommend treatment.
  • Test accessibility, language quality, and non-digital alternatives.
  • Provide a direct, transparent route to human assistance.
