USE CASE 03

Public inquiry response assistant

Help contact-center and program staff prepare consistent answers from approved public health content, with citations and clear escalation.

Matched code acceleratorConstituent Services AgentOpen GitHub repository

Business need

Make approved answers easier to deliver.

Public health hotlines and program offices answer recurring questions about vaccination, testing, reportable conditions, environmental health, and local services. Staff must respond quickly while using the current approved message and recognizing questions that require clinical or program escalation.

This pattern retrieves approved content, drafts a response with citations, shows confidence, and keeps the staff member in control. It supports assisted service; it does not independently provide clinical advice or make eligibility decisions.

  • Shorter handling time for routine inquiries
  • More consistent use of current approved language
  • Earlier escalation of urgent, clinical, or uncertain questions

Data involved

Use a small, controlled answer base.

Approved messages

FAQs, health advisories, fact sheets, scripts, referral instructions, service descriptions, and program-specific response templates.

Service information

Program contacts, clinic locations and hours, appointment requirements, language access, accessibility accommodations, and escalation channels.

Interaction data

Question category, content retrieved, response accepted or edited, confidence, escalation reason, channel, and de-identified service metrics.

Operating process

Draft, verify, then respond.

01

Receive

Capture the question through phone, chat, email, or a staff-facing form.

02

Understand

Identify topic, urgency, language, location, and possible escalation triggers.

03

Retrieve

Find the current approved answer and relevant service information.

04

Review

Show a cited draft for staff confirmation, editing, or escalation.

05

Learn

Use unresolved questions to improve content, training, and service design.

Applied scenarios

Common assisted-service moments.

SCENARIO 01

Vaccine access

A hotline specialist receives a cited draft with eligibility guidance, nearby service options, and the correct referral path.

SCENARIO 02

Exposure question

The assistant detects a potentially urgent clinical question and routes it to the established nurse or epidemiology escalation channel.

SCENARIO 03

Environmental complaint

Program staff use the current intake requirements and jurisdiction routing guidance to help a resident file the right complaint.

Implementation guardrails

Keep responses within approved scope.

1

Content ownership

Assign a program owner and review date to every answer source.

2

Urgency detection

Define explicit escalation for emergencies, clinical advice, self-harm, abuse, and other sensitive topics.

3

Language validation

Use qualified human review for translated and culturally adapted response content.

4

Minimum data

Do not collect personal information unless it is required for the service and handled under approved policy.
