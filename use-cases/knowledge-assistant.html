USE CASE 02 · SURVEILLANCE & INFORMATICS

Epidemiology knowledge assistant

Retrieve current, cited guidance from trusted epidemiology sources without replacing professional judgment or official case determination.

Related Azure AI App TemplateRAG chat with Azure AI Search + Python

Answers questions about uploaded documents with retrieval and citations.

View template

Business need

Staff lose time finding the right version of case definitions, protocols, reporting rules, and code sets. A cited retrieval assistant makes approved evidence easier to locate and compare.

Data involved

Case definitions, surveillance manuals, investigation guides, advisories, code sets, FAQs, and metadata for owner, approval, effective date, jurisdiction, sensitivity, and source URL.

01Curate

Approve content and remove old versions.

02Index

Retain source metadata and permissions.

03Retrieve

Find relevant passages with hybrid search.

04Respond

Answer with citations and effective dates.

05Improve

Review gaps, feedback, and search quality.

Applied scenarios

  • Retrieve current classification criteria before an epidemiologist decides case status.
  • Guide a new investigator through required interviews and specimens.
  • Find approved mappings between laboratory terminology and surveillance fields.

Required guardrails

  • Search only content approved for operational use.
  • Show source, passage, owner, and effective date.
  • Apply role-based access before retrieval.
  • Do not finalize classifications or clinical actions.
