USE CASE 07 · PREPAREDNESS & GOVERNANCE

Surveillance policy and protocol review

Prepare policies and protocols for qualified review by finding relevant evidence, changed language, and possible gaps.

Related Azure AI App TemplateDocument Generation and Summarization

Finds relevant documents and summarizes unstructured information. It is a starting point, not a compliance checker.

Open GitHub repository View catalog entry

Imagine when a new respiratory surveillance feed is ready, but the operating protocol is not.

The program manager must align data collection, access, retention, incident response, quality review, and partner responsibilities before governance reviewers will approve production use.

Business need and data challenge

The evidence is distributed across the draft SOP, data-use agreement, system-security plan, records schedule, privacy requirements, accessibility standards, and earlier approved protocols. Definitions and control language may differ across documents or versions.

The business need is a traceable review package that highlights possible gaps and changed language. AI prepares evidence; qualified reviewers decide.

01Collect

Draft, agreements, controls, and review standards

02Parse

Sections, tables, definitions, and versions

03Compare

Requirements, precedents, and changed language

04Review

Program, privacy, security, legal, and records

05Approve

Resolve findings and preserve the decision record

How this applies across the broader workflow

The review workspace ingests the Word or PDF protocol, related data-use agreement, security controls, retention schedule, and approved predecessor documents. It extracts headings and defined terms, identifies referenced systems and roles, and compares provisions such as purpose, minimum necessary access, data quality, audit, incident response, retention, destruction, and termination.

Each potential issue is tied to the exact draft section and supporting source. Document owners respond in a review matrix, while privacy, security, legal, accessibility, records, and program reviewers accept, reject, or revise the finding. The final protocol, disposition log, and approval evidence remain separate records.

Data challenges to resolve

  • Multiple sources of authority with different owners, dates, scope, and precedence.
  • Requirements embedded in tables, appendices, cross-references, scans, and inconsistent defined terms.
  • Separating evidence preparation from qualified legal, privacy, security, records, and program judgment.

Required guardrails

  • Use only authoritative, versioned review sources.
  • Do not present AI output as legal approval or compliance certification.
  • Show the underlying document text and supporting rule.
  • Preserve reviewed drafts, findings, and approvals as separate records.

Reflection questions

  • Which review sources are authoritative and current?
  • Who owns each finding and final disposition?
  • How will unresolved risks block production release?
