USE CASE 07

Surveillance policy and protocol review

Find missing, inconsistent, or ambiguous requirements before formal program, privacy, security, legal, accessibility, and records review.

Matched code acceleratorPolicy Compliance CheckerOpen GitHub repository

Business need

Find review issues before the approval cycle.

Surveillance protocols, data-use agreements, standard operating procedures, and technical policies must align with program rules and multiple governance requirements. Missing retention language, unclear access roles, or inconsistent approval steps can create repeated review cycles.

This pattern parses a draft, applies configurable rules and AI-assisted analysis, identifies the relevant section, ranks the finding, and proposes a question or revision for the document owner. It prepares review; it does not provide legal approval or compliance certification.

  • Earlier discovery of missing and inconsistent requirements
  • Traceable findings tied to source rules and document sections
  • More focused use of program, privacy, security, and legal reviewers

Data involved

Separate the document from the rules.

Documents under review

Surveillance protocols, data-use agreements, standard operating procedures, system plans, notices, forms, and implementation guidance.

Review rules

Program requirements, privacy and security controls, retention schedules, accessibility standards, approval rules, and required clauses.

Findings and decisions

Document section, matched rule, severity, rationale, recommendation, owner response, reviewer decision, version, and disposition.

Operating process

Create an auditable review queue.

01

Parse

Extract document structure, text, tables, headings, and version metadata.

02

Check

Apply deterministic rules first, then bounded AI analysis for context.

03

Explain

Tie each finding to the document section and authoritative review rule.

04

Review

Document owners and qualified reviewers accept, reject, or revise findings.

05

Record

Retain the reviewed version, decisions, and evidence under records policy.

Applied scenarios

Documents the pattern can prepare for review.

SCENARIO 01

Surveillance procedure

A new syndromic surveillance procedure is checked for retention, access, audit, escalation, and approval language.

SCENARIO 02

Data-use agreement

The system flags missing purpose, minimum-necessary, redisclosure, breach, destruction, and termination provisions for qualified review.

SCENARIO 03

Protocol revision

Two versions are compared to identify changed responsibilities, thresholds, timelines, and governance requirements.

Implementation guardrails

Do not confuse assistance with approval.

1

Authoritative rule set

Every check needs an owner, source, scope, version, and effective date.

2

Qualified review

Program, legal, privacy, security, accessibility, and records reviewers retain approval authority.

3

Explainable findings

Do not issue a score or recommendation without showing the underlying text and rule.

4

Version control

Preserve the reviewed draft, final document, findings, dispositions, and approvals as separate records.
