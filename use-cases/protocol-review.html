USE CASE 07 · PREPAREDNESS & GOVERNANCE

Surveillance policy and protocol review

Prepare policies and protocols for qualified review by finding relevant evidence, changed language, and possible gaps.

Related Azure AI App TemplateDocument Generation and Summarization

Finds relevant documents and summarizes unstructured information. It is a starting point, not a compliance checker.

View template

Business need

Draft protocols and agreements can contain missing, inconsistent, or superseded requirements. AI can prepare evidence and comparisons, but program, privacy, security, legal, accessibility, and records reviewers retain approval authority.

Data involved

Protocols, standard procedures, data-use agreements, retention schedules, approved policy rules, document sections, versions, findings, owner responses, reviewer decisions, and final dispositions.

01Parse

Extract document structure and version data.

02Retrieve

Find relevant approved rules and precedents.

03Compare

Highlight changed or missing language.

04Review

Qualified owners accept or reject findings.

05Record

Retain versions, evidence, and decisions.

Applied scenarios

  • Prepare a syndromic surveillance procedure for governance review.
  • Find missing purpose, retention, or termination language in a data-use agreement.
  • Compare protocol versions for changed thresholds, timelines, and responsibilities.

Required guardrails

  • Use only authoritative, versioned review sources.
  • Do not present AI output as legal approval or compliance certification.
  • Show the underlying document text and supporting rule.
  • Preserve reviewed drafts, findings, and approvals as separate records.
