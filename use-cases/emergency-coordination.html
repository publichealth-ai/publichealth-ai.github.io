USE CASE 05 · PREPAREDNESS & GOVERNANCE

Public health emergency coordination

Assemble a shared operational picture and draft coordinated plans across surveillance, clinical, logistics, communications, and partner teams.

Related Azure AI App TemplateHealthcare Agent Orchestrator

Coordinates specialized agents across healthcare data and multidisciplinary workflows; adaptation is required for incident management.

View template

Business need

Response teams must reconcile health signals, clinical capacity, staffing, supplies, communications, and partner status under time pressure. Agents can prepare bounded analyses; incident command retains authority.

Data involved

Surveillance and laboratory signals, hospital capacity, staffing and supplies, weather, maps, facilities, partner status, plans, playbooks, incident logs, and after-action reports.

01Frame

Set objectives, authority, and data snapshot.

02Assess

Run bounded specialist analyses.

03Reconcile

Expose gaps, conflicts, and dependencies.

04Approve

Command revises and authorizes actions.

05Update

Refresh data and record decisions.

Applied scenarios

  • Combine heat signals, forecasts, cooling centers, and outreach capacity.
  • Coordinate surveillance, laboratory, clinical, vaccination, and communications workstreams.
  • Identify service continuity and supply risks during severe weather.

Required guardrails

  • Require authorized approval for every operational action.
  • Show the source and age of material data.
  • Plan for missing feeds, contradictions, and network disruption.
  • Exercise the workflow before operational deployment.
