USE CASE 05 · PREPAREDNESS & GOVERNANCE

Public health emergency coordination

Assemble a shared operational picture and draft coordinated plans across surveillance, clinical, logistics, communications, and partner teams.

Related Azure AI App TemplateHealthcare Agent Orchestrator

Coordinates specialized agents across healthcare data and multidisciplinary workflows; adaptation is required for incident management.

Open GitHub repository View catalog entry

Imagine when a dangerous heat event is forecast as emergency visits begin to rise.

The response team must decide where to extend cooling-center hours, which neighborhoods need outreach, whether hospitals are under pressure, and what message should reach the public before the next operational briefing.

Business need and data challenge

Relevant signals arrive at different speeds and geographic levels: National Weather Service forecasts, syndromic emergency-department data, EMS calls, hospital capacity, 211 requests, cooling-center status, transit access, and community partner updates.

The business need is a time-stamped common operating picture that exposes uncertainty and dependencies. It is not autonomous incident command.

01Sense

Weather, health, EMS, facility, and community feeds

02Assess

Health impact, capacity, geography, and equity

03Plan

Resource, outreach, logistics, and message options

04Approve

Incident command reviews and authorizes

05Operate

Assign actions, monitor, and update the plan

How this applies across the broader workflow

A weather agent summarizes forecast thresholds and affected time periods. A surveillance agent analyzes heat-related syndrome counts and rates from the syndromic platform. Resource and logistics agents compare cooling-center hours, staffing, water and transportation capacity, while a geographic analysis identifies neighborhoods with high exposure and limited access.

The orchestrator produces a draft situation summary with source timestamps, recommended questions, resource gaps, and alternative actions. Incident command validates the analysis, authorizes operational assignments, and records decisions. The next cycle incorporates updated ED visits, EMS demand, facility status, outreach completion, and public inquiries.

Data challenges to resolve

  • Different refresh cycles, geographic units, denominators, and thresholds across operational feeds.
  • Incomplete partner status, manual facility updates, and delayed confirmation of local actions.
  • Conflicting values that require source attribution, timestamps, assumptions, and uncertainty.

Required guardrails

  • Require authorized approval for every operational action.
  • Show the source and age of material data.
  • Plan for missing feeds, contradictions, and network disruption.
  • Exercise the workflow before operational deployment.

Reflection questions

  • Which health, weather, capacity, and equity thresholds trigger activation or escalation, and how will the workflow distinguish a planning signal from an approved operational trigger?
  • Who owns each operational feed, expected refresh time, and data-quality check, and what alternate source will be used when local status or partner data is late or missing?
  • Which recommendations must remain advisory until command, clinical, communications, procurement, or legal approval, and how will those decisions and dissenting views be recorded?
