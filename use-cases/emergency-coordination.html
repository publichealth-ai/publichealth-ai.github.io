USE CASE 05

Public health emergency coordination

Assemble a shared operational picture and draft coordinated plans across surveillance, clinical, logistics, communications, and partner teams.

Matched code acceleratorEmergency Response AgentOpen GitHub repository

Business need

Coordinate faster without automating command.

Public health emergencies require rapid synthesis of health signals, partner status, clinical capacity, staffing, supplies, communications, and environmental conditions. Teams often build separate views and reconcile them during high-pressure operational periods.

A multi-agent pattern can assign bounded analysis tasks to specialist components and assemble a draft response plan. Incident command and accountable leaders review assumptions, resolve conflicts, approve actions, and remain responsible for all operational decisions.

  • Faster preparation of shared situation and resource views
  • Explicit dependencies, assumptions, and unresolved decisions
  • More consistent updates across operational planning cycles

Data involved

Build from authoritative operational feeds.

Health intelligence

Surveillance trends, laboratory signals, emergency visits, hospital capacity, mortality, vulnerable-population indicators, and geographic patterns.

Operations

Staffing, supplies, vaccines or medications, clinics, shelters, transportation, partner status, communications channels, and mutual-aid resources.

Context and history

Weather, environmental hazards, jurisdiction maps, plans, playbooks, prior incident logs, after-action reports, and decision records.

Operating process

Use agents as bounded planning roles.

01

Frame

Define the operational period, objectives, decision authority, and trusted data snapshot.

02

Assess

Run bounded health, resource, logistics, communications, and equity analyses.

03

Reconcile

Identify conflicting assumptions, dependencies, gaps, and decisions required.

04

Approve

Incident command reviews the draft and authorizes, rejects, or revises actions.

05

Update

Record decisions, refresh data, and generate the next operational-period draft.

Applied scenarios

Events that benefit from coordinated synthesis.

SCENARIO 01

Heat emergency

Teams combine health signals, weather, cooling-center capacity, outreach resources, and transportation access for command review.

SCENARIO 02

Large outbreak

Surveillance, laboratory, clinical, vaccination, communications, and partner workstreams contribute to a single draft action plan.

SCENARIO 03

Severe weather disruption

The plan highlights facility closures, supply risks, continuity needs, and populations that may lose access to essential services.

Implementation guardrails

Protect command accountability.

1

Human authorization

No resource movement, alert, public message, or operational action should execute without authorized approval.

2

Time-stamped evidence

Show the data snapshot, source, and age behind every material planning statement.

3

Failure modes

Plan for missing feeds, contradictory information, stale data, model unavailability, and network disruption.

4

Exercise before use

Validate roles, escalation, outputs, and failure behavior through drills before operational deployment.
