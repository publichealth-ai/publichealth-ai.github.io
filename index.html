A practical review framework

Start with the public outcome.

Every use case should be evaluated in context before procurement, development, or deployment.

1

People

Define who benefits, who may be burdened, and where qualified human review remains essential.

2

Process

Map the current workflow, decision rights, escalation paths, and measurable public health outcome.

3

Data

Assess quality, representativeness, consent, privacy, retention, access, and appropriate reuse.

4

Technology

Test accuracy, security, accessibility, interoperability, monitoring, and failure behavior.

Important: This inventory is illustrative, not implementation or legal guidance. Agencies should complete privacy, security, civil rights, accessibility, clinical, records, and procurement reviews appropriate to each use case.
